Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

This policy explains how the games published by Aissam Ihaddadene ("we") handle information: Gemgrid. Contact: aissam.0412@gmail.com.

In short

  • No account is needed. Your progress, scores and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not run any server and we do not sell your data.
  • The games are free and show ads. Ads, usage statistics and crash reports are provided by the third-party services listed below, which process some data from your device.
  • You decide: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown at first launch, and you can change your choices at any time in the game's Settings › Privacy choices. Ad tracking also requires your permission in the iOS prompt ("Allow tracking").

Information stored on your device

Game progress, best scores, daily challenge results and streaks, and settings (sound, vibrations, reminder) are saved locally. They are never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data (it may remain in your own device backups).

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device: no push server is involved.

Information processed by third-party services

Advertising

Ads are served through AppLovin MAX, which works with the ad networks Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads and Mintegral. To show and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, these services may process: the advertising identifier of your device (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), the identifier for vendors (IDFV), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, operating system, language, time zone, connection type), and interactions with ads (impressions, clicks, video completions).

Without your consent (or if you refuse tracking), ads are not personalized: they are chosen from the context, such as your country or language.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the games are used (for example games started and finished, scores, daily challenge results, ads and purchases shown) in order to improve them. It processes an app instance identifier, device information, approximate location derived from the IP address and these events. It may use the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. Statistics are viewed in aggregated form and kept for at most 14 months.

Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics collects technical data when the game crashes (stack trace, device model, operating system version, free memory and a random installation identifier) so we can fix bugs. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details; the game only receives Apple's confirmation of the purchase.

Sharing

When you share a daily result, the text and image go to the app you choose, through the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared without your action.

Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where the law requires it, personalized advertising and access to your device information rely on your consent, collected through a certified consent management platform (Google User Messaging Platform, IAB TCF). Crash reports, security, fraud prevention and the delivery of non-personalized ads rely on our legitimate interest in offering a free, stable game. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings › Privacy choices, and tracking permission in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Partners and their privacy policies

  • AppLovin (AppLovin MAX) — advertising and ad mediation
  • Google AdMob — advertising and consent form (Google UMP)
  • Meta Audience Network — advertising
  • Unity Ads — advertising
  • Mintegral — advertising
  • Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics — usage statistics and crash reports

These services may process data outside your country, including in the United States, with the safeguards provided by law (such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to its processing, withdraw consent and request portability. Because we do not keep any data that identifies you, the simplest way is to change your choices in the game and in iOS settings, delete the game, or contact the partners above. You can also write to us at aissam.0412@gmail.com and we will help. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Children

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has used our games and data was collected, contact us.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, with a new date at the top.

Politique de confidentialité

Cette politique explique comment les jeux édités par Aissam Ihaddadene (« nous ») traitent les informations : Gemgrid. Contact : aissam.0412@gmail.com.

En bref

  • Aucun compte n'est nécessaire. Ta progression, tes scores et tes réglages sont enregistrés uniquement sur ton appareil.

Informations enregistrées sur ton appareil

La progression, les meilleurs scores, les résultats et séries du défi du jour et les réglages (son, vibrations, rappel) sont enregistrés localement. Ils ne nous sont jamais envoyés. Supprimer le jeu supprime ces données (elles peuvent rester dans tes propres sauvegardes de l'appareil).

Les rappels quotidiens sont des notifications locales programmées sur ton appareil : aucun serveur de notifications n'est utilisé.

Informations traitées par des services tiers

Publicité

Les publicités passent par AppLovin MAX, qui travaille avec les régies Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads et Mintegral. Pour afficher et mesurer les publicités, limiter leur répétition et lutter contre la fraude, ces services peuvent traiter : l'identifiant publicitaire de ton appareil (IDFA, seulement si tu autorises le suivi), l'identifiant fournisseur (IDFV), ton adresse IP et la localisation approximative qui en découle, des informations sur l'appareil et l'app (modèle, système, langue, fuseau horaire, type de connexion) et tes interactions avec les publicités (affichages, clics, vidéos terminées).

Statistiques d'utilisation

Nous utilisons Google Analytics pour Firebase pour comprendre comment les jeux sont utilisés (par exemple parties commencées et terminées, scores, résultats du défi du jour, publicités et achats proposés) afin de les améliorer. Le service traite un identifiant d'installation, des informations sur l'appareil, une localisation approximative déduite de l'adresse IP et ces événements. Il ne peut utiliser l'identifiant publicitaire que si tu autorises le suivi. Les statistiques sont consultées sous forme agrégée et conservées au maximum 14 mois.

Rapports de plantage

Firebase Crashlytics collecte des données techniques quand le jeu plante (trace de l'erreur, modèle d'appareil, version du système, mémoire libre et un identifiant d'installation aléatoire) pour nous permettre de corriger les bugs. Les rapports sont conservés 90 jours.

Achats

L'achat « Sans pub » est entièrement géré par Apple. Nous ne recevons jamais tes informations de paiement ; le jeu reçoit seulement la confirmation de l'achat par Apple.

Partage

Quand tu partages un résultat du défi du jour, le texte et l'image vont vers l'app que tu choisis, via le menu de partage d'iOS. Rien n'est partagé sans ton action.

Bases légales (EEE, Royaume-Uni, Suisse)

Les rapports de plantage, la sécurité, la lutte contre la fraude et l'affichage de publicités non personnalisées reposent sur notre intérêt légitime à proposer un jeu gratuit et stable. Tu peux retirer ton consentement à tout moment dans Réglages › Choix de confidentialité, et l'autorisation de suivi dans Réglages › Confidentialité et sécurité › Suivi d'iOS.
